KHUNG NGU CANH DU AN WEBSITE

1. NGUOI HOC
- Toi la nguoi moi hoc lap trinh web.
- Huong dan bang tieng Viet, ro rang va de hieu.
- Giai thich nguyen nhan loi truoc khi de xuat cach sua.

2. WEBSITE
- Ten website: Phu tung Honda Tam.
- Muc dich: Gioi thieu cua hang va cac san pham phu tung, nhot, linh kien danh cho xe Honda.
- Doi tuong: Nguoi di xe Honda va tho sua xe.
- Thong tin dia chi, so dien thoai va email chua duoc cung cap; giu placeholder, khong tu tao thong tin.
- Day la website tinh, khong co dang nhap, gio hang, thanh toan, co so du lieu hoac backend.

3. CONG NGHE
- Chi su dung HTML5 va CSS3 thuan; JavaScript thuan chi khi that su can thiet.
- Khong su dung React, Vue, Angular, Next.js, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind can cai dat, npm, framework, thu vien hoac cong cu build.
- Khong yeu cau cai them thu vien.

5. QUY TAC TRA LOI VA SUA DOI
- Moi lan chi thuc hien buoc tiep theo sau khi buoc truoc da duoc xac nhan.
- Neu yeu cau chua ro, hoi toi da 4 cau lam ro.
- Khong tu y them chuc nang hoac thay doi cong nghe.
- Moi lan sua, chi sua dung phan duoc yeu cau va khong lam hong phan dang hoat dong.
- Khi gui tep code, ghi ten tep truoc va gui day du noi dung tep, khong viet tat hoac bo phan con lai.
- Sau khi gui code, giai thich ngan gon cac phan quan trong.
- Neu toi gui anh man hinh, phan tich anh truoc khi de xuat sua.
- Neu toi gui loi Console/DevTools, dua vao loi thuc te, khong doan.
- Khi kiem thu, liet ke van de truoc; chua sua cho den khi toi chon van de.

6. QUY TAC HTML, CSS VA DIEU HUONG
- Trang chu bat buoc la public/index.html.
- CSS dung chung dat tai public/css/style.css.
- Moi trang HTML dung <link rel="stylesheet" href="css/style.css">.
- HTML phai co meta charset UTF-8 va meta viewport.
- Ten tep HTML viet thuong, khong dau, khong khoang trang.
- Cac trang dung chung phong cach, header, menu, footer va style.css.
- Menu phai lien ket dung den cac trang hien co.
- Giao dien responsive, khong tran ngang tren dien thoai.
- Noi dung tieng Viet co dau; anh co alt mo ta.
